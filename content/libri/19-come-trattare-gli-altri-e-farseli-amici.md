Il classico senza tempo sulle relazioni umane: non manipolazione, ma empatia, ascolto e rispetto.
Carnegie insegna a non criticare, a dare apprezzamento sincero, a interessarsi davvero agli altri e a parlare dei loro bisogni.
Mostra come evitare discussioni, ammettere i propri errori, lasciare che l’altro salvi la faccia e si convinca da sé.

--- en ---
The timeless classic on human relations: not manipulation, but empathy, listening, and respect.
Carnegie teaches not to criticize, to give honest and sincere appreciation, to become genuinely interested in others, and to talk in terms of their needs.
He shows how to avoid arguments, admit your own mistakes, let the other person save face, and let them convince themselves.
A practical manual to win friends, influence people, and lead without resentment, in work and life.
